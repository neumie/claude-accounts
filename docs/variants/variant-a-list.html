Variant A — Account list

Classic and minimal. A flat list of saved accounts is the whole popup: monogram, email, org names, last used. The active account gets the raised stripe, an accent check and the word "Active"; every other healthy row is itself the switch button, so switching is one click with nothing revealed on hover. Artifact context (auto-switched, or asking first) appears as one inline notice above the list and points at a row in it. Auto-switch is a small Auto / Ask / Off group at the bottom, with remembered artifacts one push away. Every frame has exactly one filled button: normally "Add account" in the pinned footer; when claude.ai holds a session that is not saved yet, "Save current account" takes over and "Add account" drops to quiet.
